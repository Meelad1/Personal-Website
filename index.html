About Me

My responsibilities included testing user stories for quoting systems, documenting test completion, reporting and tracking bugs until resolution, and participating in Scrum ceremonies, including daily stand-ups, refinements, planning, and retrospectives.

As I progress in my graduate studies, I am actively seeking opportunities to bring my knowledge and skills to the workforce. I am open to work and excited to contribute to innovative projects as part of a dynamic and forward-thinking team.
